Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our cleaning services

What cleaning services does Bear Carpet Care offer?

Bear Carpet Care offers professional carpet cleaning, upholstery cleaning, and oriental and area rug cleaning. Services include deep steam cleaning, pet stain and odor removal, Scotchgard protection, sanitizing, and rug repair and restoration.

Where is Bear Carpet Care located?

Bear Carpet Care is based in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania and serves homes and businesses across Central PA, including York, Lancaster, Lebanon, and Hershey.

Are your cleaning products safe for pets and children?

Yes. We use non-toxic, hypoallergenic, biodegradable cleaning solutions on every job. No harsh chemicals or harmful residues are left behind, so they are safe for the whole family.

How many years of experience does Bear Carpet Care have?

Bear Carpet Care has over 30 years of professional cleaning experience serving homes and businesses in Harrisburg, PA and the surrounding area.

Do you offer free pick-up and delivery for rug cleaning?

Yes. We offer free pick-up and delivery for all area rug cleaning orders. We handle your rugs with care from your door to our cleaning facility and back.
