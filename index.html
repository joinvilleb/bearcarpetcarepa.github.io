Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our cleaning services

What cleaning services does Bear Carpet Care offer?

We offer professional carpet cleaning, upholstery cleaning, and oriental and area rug cleaning. Services include deep steam cleaning, pet stain and odor removal, Scotchgard protection, sanitizing, and rug repair and restoration.

Are your cleaning products safe for pets and children?

Yes. We use non-toxic, hypoallergenic, biodegradable cleaning solutions on every job. No harsh chemicals or harmful residues are left behind — safe for the whole family.

How many years of experience does Bear Carpet Care have?

Bear Carpet Care has over 30 years of professional cleaning experience serving homes and businesses in Harrisburg, PA and the surrounding area.

Do you offer free pick-up and delivery for rug cleaning?

Yes. We offer free pick-up and delivery for all area rug cleaning orders. We handle your rugs with care from your door to our cleaning facility and back.

How do I get a quote?

Call us at (717) 454-7347 or use our contact form. Quotes are free with no obligation.
